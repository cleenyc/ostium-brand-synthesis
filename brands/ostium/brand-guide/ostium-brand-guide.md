# Ostium Brand Guide

Derived from `brands/ostium/brand.md` v0.3 portability revision.

## Essence

Ostium is the onchain gateway to global markets: precise, liquid, self-custodial, transparent, and trader-native.

## Visual system

- Foundation: dark charcoal / near-black.
- Atmosphere: burnt orange, copper, red-orange heat, glow, gradient, or print-like blocks.
- Text: off-white and muted warm gray.
- Market semantics: green for long/up/profit; red/pink for short/down/loss.
- Product proof: charts, order tickets, open positions, fees, spread/slippage, price impact, PnL, trade records.

## Social asset templates

Use one of the observed Ostium-native templates rather than a generic abstract launch poster:

1. **Campaign/event banner** — 16:9/wide, full-bleed market/news/technology imagery, large 1–3 word headline, muted subtitle, small white logo bottom-left, circular asset icons bottom-right.
2. **Cost breakdown card** — square/portrait orange-red gradient, top-left logo, question headline with the dollar amount as dominant line, trade descriptor row, fee table, total-cost callout.
3. **Trade proof card** — 16:9, asset-specific cinematic background, huge notional number, concise context line, black PnL module, small logo bottom-center.
4. **Product-position overlay** — square, trading floor or gritty macro collage background, centered black product UI module, position cards, bottom-center logo.
5. **Briefing card** — 4:5, dark brown/orange gradient, subject header, stacked market/Ostium data sections, thin dividers, bottom-center logo.

## Copy voice

Write short, exact, product-led lines.

Good patterns:

- “Hot Print.”
- “2x Points This Week.”
- “How much does a $9,699,990 trade cost on Ostium?”
- “$75,908,000 in WTI shorts.”
- “New record. Largest US100 long in Ostium history.”

Avoid vague hype, investment advice, fake partner claims, generic future-of-finance language, and internal/process copy in public examples.

## Layout rules

- Start with the market event or trade proof, not abstract geometry.
- Make the number, question, campaign phrase, or product UI module the focal point.
- Keep the Ostium logo restrained as a signature.
- Enforce safe-area wrapping and font-size reduction for long headlines.
- Keep data modules readable at social-preview size.

## Portability rules

- For unsupported asset types, construct from principles: market-specific context, one proof-led message, one credible product/data module, orange/black atmosphere, and restrained logo use.
- Preserve semantic color discipline across tools: green for long/up/profit, red/pink for short/down/loss, orange for brand/action/heat.
- Treat renderer coordinates, exact spacing multipliers, and placeholder geometry as implementation details, not brand truth.
- Validate new asset types by checking recognizability, market specificity, semantic color use, logo restraint, and absence of unsupported claims.

## Open decisions

Full logo rules, exact font licensing, full media-kit constraints, and redistribution treatment for sampled social media should be reviewed before treating this as a final production brand guide.
